# SSG24 — 3D Printable Files & Community Designs

This directory is structured to house print-ready STL meshes (`stl/`) and editable CAD source files (`source/`) for the SSG24 and Modify MOD24 platform.

## Community 3D Model Directory (via Yeggi / Thingiverse / Cults3D)

For community-designed 3D printable parts and accessories hosted on external repositories, use the aggregated search directories below:

- **[Master Yeggi SSG24 Search Index](https://www.yeggi.com/q/ssg24/)** — Aggregated search index tracking SSG24 3D models across Thingiverse, Cults3D, Printables, and Thangs.
- **Magazine Holders & Magwells**: Search for **"SSG24 magazine holder"** or **"SSG24 magwell"** on Yeggi/Thingiverse for low-profile magwells, belt clip pouches, and recessed magazine protection sleeves.
- **Speedloader Adapters**: Search for **"SSG24 speedloader adapter"** for custom loading nozzles that lock onto SSG24 magazine feed lips to prevent BB spillage during rapid reloading.
- **Scope Killflashes & Lens Protectors**: Search for **"Novritsch scope killflash"** or **"airsoft scope flashkiller"** on Cults3D/Yeggi for 40mm and 50mm honeycomb anti-reflection shields.
- **Tactical Chassis & Bipod Mounts**: Search for **"SSG24 chassis"** or **"SSG24 bipod mount"** for full chassis conversion projects (such as the Akura Tactical Precision Chassis) and MLOK/bipod adapters.

## Printing Notes & Recommendations

- **Material**: For functional mechanical parts (such as magazine holders, magwells, and bipod mounts), print in **PETG**, **ASA**, or **ABS** rather than PLA to withstand field impacts and summer outdoor heat.
- **Infill & Walls**: For high-stress adapters or speedloader nozzles, use at least 4–6 perimeter walls and 35%+ gyroid infill for dimensional stability and strength.
