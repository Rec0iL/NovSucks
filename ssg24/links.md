# SSG24 — Alternative Sources

- [Tridos Design — SSG24 / MOD24 Upgrades](https://tridos.design/) — Premium aftermarket design hub specializing in precision 3D/CNC upgrades for the SSG24 and Modify MOD24. Famous for the **TDC 2.0 Hop-up upgrade**, **M4 magazine adapters**, **SORA R-Hop buckings**, and protective trigger dust covers.
  - Search term if the link dies: **"Tridos Design SSG24 TDC"** or **"Tridos MOD24 upgrades"**
- [Modify MOD24 Factory Replacement Parts (via Begadi / Airsoft Retailers)](https://www.begadi.com/) — Modify-Tech is the original OEM manufacturer of the SSG24 internals. Searching for MOD24 factory spare parts yields identical cylinders, zero triggers, pistons, and hop-up units without rebrand markups.
  - Search term if the link dies: **"Modify MOD24 spare parts"** or **"Modify MOD24X upgrade parts"**
- [Rapax Springs — APS-2 / MOD24 Sniper Springs](https://www.skirmshop.nl/) — Specialized European airsoft spring manufacturer producing high-grade, low-tolerance sniper springs calibrated specifically for SSG24 / APS-2 piston diameters (2J, 3J+, and custom ratings).
  - Search term if the link dies: **"Rapax spring SSG24"** or **"Rapax APS-2 sniper spring"**
- [AirsoftPro APS-2 / MOD24 Upgrade CNC Parts](https://airsoftpro.cz/) — Czech manufacturer producing CNC trigger sears, zero triggers, steel spring guides, and hybrid pistons compatible with APS-2 and Modify MOD24 systems.
  - Search term if the link dies: **"AirsoftPro Modify MOD24 parts"** or **"AirsoftPro APS-2 upgrade"**
- [CYMA / Snow Wolf M24 Magazines on AliExpress](https://www.aliexpress.com/) — Inexpensive 30rd M24 magazines that can serve as budget replacements for SSG24 magazines. May require minor sanding/filing on the upper locking lip to click smoothly into an SSG24 magwell.
  - Search term if the link dies: **"CYMA M24 magazine airsoft"** or **"Snow Wolf M24 magazine"**
