# SSG10

Novritsch SSG10 (A1, A2, A3 variants) — bolt-action airsoft sniper rifle. The SSG10 is built on the industry-standard **Tokyo Marui VSR-10 / Jing Gong (JG) BAR-10** architecture, featuring upgraded internals primarily OEM'd by **Modify-Tech** (similar to Modify MOD24 / VSR specifications).

## Known quirks / notes

- **VSR-10 Compatibility**: Highly compatible with standard VSR-10 aftermarket parts (cylinders, pistons, springs, spring guides, hop-up buckings, and inner barrels).
- **Trigger System**: Uses a 90-degree trigger box and a 90-degree piston lip (standard VSR upgrade spec). Do not install older 45-degree TM/JG pistons or trigger sears without converting both components.
- **Hop-Up & Barrel**: Uses standard VSR-10 / GBB cut buckings and inner barrels. The stock bucking is an open-window design compatible with Maple Leaf and Stalker SR/R-Hop buckings.
- **Cylinder & Receiver Tolerances**: While VSR-10 compatible, the outer diameter of the SSG10 cylinder and inner glide rings have tight tolerances; some aftermarket VSR cylinders may bind if glide rings aren't sanded slightly.
- **Top-Tier Upgrades (Stalker / Skirmshop)**: The **Stalker** lineup (available via Skirmshop) offers dedicated drop-in upgrade components engineered specifically for the SSG10 and VSR-10 platforms, including the **Kraken TDC Hop-up Chamber**, **Scorpion 2nd Gen Piston & Spring Guide**, **Double Mute Cylinder Head**, and **Morpheus Dual Bore barrels**.
- **Magazines**: Uses standard VSR-10 style magazines. Novritsch SSG10 magazines work in most JG BAR-10 / TM VSR-10 receivers, but some third-party VSR mags may require minor filing on the magazine catch lip to click securely into the SSG10 stock.
